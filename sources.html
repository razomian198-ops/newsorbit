About our sources

How NewsOrbit works

NewsOrbit collects headlines automatically from the official sources listed below. For each item we show the headline, a short note from the source’s own feed (at most 200 characters), the source name, the date, and a link to the original report. We do not copy full articles.

NewsOrbit has not independently verified these reports. Please read the original source for the full story.

Federal Register (Research page)

The Research page shows documents from the Federal Register, the official daily journal of the US federal government (rules, proposed rules, notices and presidential documents). They come from its public data service. These documents are not the full text of laws passed by Congress, and NewsOrbit does not give legal advice. We do not use the official seals or logos of the Office of the Federal Register or the National Archives. NewsOrbit is not affiliated with the US government.
